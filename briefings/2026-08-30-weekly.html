Weekly Markets Report — 24–30 August 2026

Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's first major Jackson Hole speech (28 Aug) called inflation "concerning" and said the Fed "has more work to do," flipping market expectations from rate cuts toward a real chance of a September hike (implied odds jumped ~35% → 57%). Nvidia posted a blowout Q2 FY2027 (revenue $96.2bn, +106% y/y; guided 70% FY2028 growth; stock +9%), keeping AI/tech risk appetite alive even as rate-sensitive growth names wobbled late week. Eurozone flash PMIs hit a 9-month high (led by Germany) but inflation stayed sticky (ECB's Lane: ~3% through year-end). Geopolitically: US-Canada tariff deal reduces trade risk; Gaza ceasefire called a "point of no return" risk by the US envoy; Russia-Ukraine war unresolved; China-Taiwan tensions escalating (new PRC naval base, record Taiwan defense budget). Oil ~$88-94/bbl, gold firm, USD rallied post-Warsh (EUR/USD pulled back from ~1.1676 high).
